Explainable Clinical Decision Support

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) often progresses without outward symptoms in its early stages. While standard machine learning models often operate as opaque "black boxes", this system combines a Decision Tree Classifier with SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) to provide both an accurate risk assessment and a transparent, case-by-case clinical rationale.

🔍
Transparent Reasoning: Every prediction is deconstructed into risk-elevating and protective factors.
📊
Quantitative SHAP: Explains exactly how much each laboratory biomarker pushed the probability.
🛡️
Zero Data Storage: Patient vitals are processed strictly in memory without persistent tracking.
